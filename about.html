About Mod

GregTechNightmare is a mod that adds a wide variety of content for the GTNH modpack. It aims to improve the GTNH experience by replacing pointless waiting with cool multiblocks and mechanics. Created by EvgenWarGold with his friends Crazer and Faotik. The concept is based on the Twist-Space-Technology addon. The mod is actively being improved if you find any bugs or have ideas, please open an issue on GitHub.
